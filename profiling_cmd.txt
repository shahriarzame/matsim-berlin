Identify the target JVM PID

From a terminal: jps -lvm (lists local JVMs with PIDs); or read PID from IntelliJ’s Run console (it prints like PID: 12345 on launch with some runtimes).

Linux (lightweight, live)

Overall CPU & per-thread CPU:
top -H -p <PID> → press 1 to show cores; %CPU/100 ≈ effective cores.

Process CPU/memory timeline to CSV:
pidstat -p <PID> 1 -durh > pidstat.log
